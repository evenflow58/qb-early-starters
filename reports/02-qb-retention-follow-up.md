# QB Early Starters — Retention Follow-up (247Sports Composite Classes 2022–2024)

Status as of fall 2026 (November 2026). All findings below were gathered via web-index searches read on 2026-11-08 (flagged "index" throughout; no live-browser verification was performed in this run).

## Summary

Of the 48 quarterbacks who started 6+ games in a single season in Year 1 or Year 2 of college (the corrected qualifier set — see Finding 0), only **11 of 48 (22.9%) never transferred away from the school they signed with (Metric A)**, and only **13 of 48 (27.1%) never transferred away from the school where they earned their qualifying starts (Metric B)**. In other words, roughly three of every four early-starting QBs from the 2022–2024 classes had changed schools at least once by fall 2026. Retention was lowest among 3-stars (4 of 26 stayed under both metrics) and, on the signing-school metric, nearly as low among 5-stars (2 of 7). The two metrics diverge for exactly two players — Julian Sayin and Demond Williams Jr. — who transferred before ever qualifying and then stayed at their qualifying school.

## Findings

### 0. Reconciliation of the qualifier list (48 vs. 49)

**The prior report's summary total was off by one. The corrected qualifier set is 48: 22 blue-chip (7 five-star + 15 four-star) + 26 three-star.** No omitted blue-chip qualifier could be found. The two nearest misses were checked against starts data and both fall short of the 6-start test (index, read 2026-11-08):

- **Conner Weigman (2022, 5-star, Texas A&M):** 4 starts in 2022 and 4 starts in 2023 — never 6 in Year 1 or Year 2. Not a qualifier.
- **Dante Moore (2023, 5-star, UCLA):** started 5 games in 2023. Not a qualifier.

Star assignments were verified against the 247Sports Composite: the 7 five-star qualifiers are Klubnik, Allar, Iamaleava, Arnold, Sayin, Raiola and Lagway (Lagway: 5-star Composite, No. 7 overall / No. 2 QB in 2024 per 247's final update). The other 15 blue-chippers are 4-star Composite, including CJ Bailey (No. 29 QB, 2024), Sam Leavitt, Aidan Chiles (No. 168), LaNorris Sellers (No. 256) and Marcel Reed (No. 296).

One correction to the mission's framing emerged during the research: **Aidan Chiles's qualifying school is Michigan State, not Oregon State.** Chiles made 0 starts at Oregon State in 2023 (9 appearances as a backup) and started all 12 games at Michigan State in 2024 (his Year 2). He is therefore a fifth pre-qualifying-transfer case, alongside Holstein, Leavitt, Sayin and Demond Williams Jr.

**Class-comparison caveat (as requested):** the classes are not on equal footing. By November 2026 the 2022 class has had two more portal cycles — and an NFL draft — than the 2024 class. Three 2022 qualifiers have already been drafted (Klubnik, Allar, and Mendoza, the No. 1 overall pick) and a fourth (E.J. Warner) has exhausted eligibility; those departures count as "stayed" only when they never transferred (Klubnik, Allar). The 2024 class's higher retention (5/16 and 7/16) partly reflects less elapsed time rather than greater loyalty, and its members still have the January 2027 portal window ahead of them. The 2023 class matching 2022's low rate (3/16 under both metrics) despite one less year is arguably the most striking class result.

### 4. Notable cases

**Early starters who transferred anyway despite starting:**

- **Fernando Mendoza (2022, Cal → Indiana)** — the extreme case in both directions: left Cal after two starting seasons, won the Heisman and a national title at Indiana in 2025, and was drafted No. 1 overall in 2026. A transfer that "worked," but a transfer under both metrics.
- **Nico Iamaleava (2023, Tennessee → UCLA)** — left Tennessee in spring 2025 in a public NIL dispute after starting a full season; at UCLA in 2026 until a season-ending knee injury (Oct 10, 2026); has said he plans to return to UCLA in 2027.
- **Darian Mensah (2023, Tulane → Duke → Miami)** — Duke sued over his NIL contract when he left for Miami in January 2026; the suit was settled and he is Miami's 2026 starter.
- **DJ Lagway (2024, Florida → Baylor)** and **Dylan Raiola (2024, Nebraska → Oregon)** — two of the three 2024 five-stars who qualified at their signing school both left in January 2026; Raiola landed as a backup at Oregon (starting only during Dante Moore's concussion absence), and his Nebraska job was taken by another qualifier on this list, Anthony Colandrea.
- **Brendan Sorsby (2022) — the study's only "Other."** Indiana → Cincinnati → Texas Tech (Jan 2026), then parted ways with Texas Tech on June 15, 2026 after an NCAA gambling-ineligibility ruling; the 2026 supplemental draft was not held/denied, so he has no team in fall 2026 and is preparing for the 2027 NFL Draft.
- **Serial movers:** AJ Swann is on his fourth school (Vanderbilt → LSU → Appalachian State → Mississippi State); Katin Houser, Ashton Daniels, Gio Lopez, Anthony Colandrea, Michael Van Buren Jr., Jackson Arnold, Zeon Chriss and Deshawn Purdie are each on their third.
- **Coach-followers:** Rocco Becht (Iowa State → Penn State, Matt Campbell), Byrum Brown (USF → Auburn, Alex Golesh), Ethan Grunkemeyer (Penn State → Virginia Tech, James Franklin), Aidan Chiles (Oregon State → Michigan State, Jonathan Smith), Collin Hurst (Presbyterian → West Georgia, Steve Englehart), and pre-qualifying Demond Williams Jr. (Arizona → Washington, Jedd Fisch).
- **Near-miss non-transfers:** Demond Williams Jr. signed a ~$4M deal to return to Washington in January 2026, announced a portal entry four days later, and reversed within ~2 days after Washington and the Big Ten moved to block it — he never actually transferred (Metric B = Y). Keyone Jenkins likewise entered the portal in December 2024 but withdrew and returned to FIU (not a transfer); his actual transfer to UCF came in January 2026.

**Transferred first, became a starter only at the second school (the Metric A vs. B distinction):**

- **Julian Sayin (2024)** — Alabama → Ohio State before ever playing a snap; qualified at Ohio State and is still there. Metric A: N. Metric B: Y.
- **Demond Williams Jr. (2024)** — signed with Arizona, enrolled at Washington; qualified and stayed there. A: N. B: Y.
- **Sam Leavitt (2023)** — Michigan State → Arizona State, where he qualified; then left ASU for LSU in January 2026. N under both, but his qualifying starts came at his second school.
- **Eli Holstein (2023)** — Alabama → Pitt, qualified at Pitt (10 starts in 2024); then Pitt → Virginia in January 2026. N under both.
- **Aidan Chiles (2023)** — Oregon State → Michigan State, qualified at Michigan State (12 starts in 2024); then MSU → Northwestern in January 2026. N under both. (The mission list did not flag Chiles as a pre-qualifying transfer; the starts data show he is one.)
