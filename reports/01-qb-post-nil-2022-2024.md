# QB Recruiting Outcomes by Star Rating — Classes of 2022, 2023, 2024

**Question:** What share of quarterbacks at each 247Sports Composite star level became early college starters — defined as starting 6+ games in a single season in either their first or second college season?

**Scope and definitions (as specified for this research):**
- Classes 2022, 2023, 2024. These are the three most recent classes that have completed their first two college seasons as of November 2026, so the "first two years" test can be judged completely for every player (2022 → seasons of 2022–23; 2023 → 2023–24; 2024 → 2024–25).
- Rankings: 247Sports **Composite** only, final rankings per class, position = QB (pro-style and dual-threat combined, as 247 lists them), high-school recruits in the main Composite class rankings (separate JUCO rankings excluded). Reclassified players are placed in the class they ultimately signed in (e.g., Austin Simmons and Cutter Boley, both reclassified from 2025 into 2023/2024).
- Starter test: **GS ≥ 6 in Year 1 OR Year 2**, where Year 1 = fall of the class year. Starts are summed across schools if a player transferred mid-window; transfers, position changes and non-enrollment are noted but do not reset the clock.
- Starts data: Sports-Reference CFB was the intended primary source. In practice, Sports-Reference's player season tables publish games (G) but **not games started (GS)**, so GS figures were taken from Sports-Reference game logs where usable and cross-checked against Wikipedia career-statistics tables (which carry GP/GS and are built from Sports-Reference plus school statistics), The Football Database (which carries G/GS), and official school roster bios/media guides — the cross-check route the mission specified for cases where Sports-Reference is incomplete. Every qualifier and every borderline non-qualifier (4–5 starts) was individually verified this way; the specific source used for each flagged player is noted.
- Star thresholds in the 247 Composite: 5★ ≥ 0.9834; 4★ 0.8901–0.9833; 3★ 0.7901–0.8900; 2★ ≤ 0.7900.
- **Verification basis:** all pages were read through the web index on 2026-11-08 (flagged "index" throughout). The live research browser was blocked by a bot challenge on 247Sports, so no figure is flagged "verified live."

## Summary — hit rates by star level (all three classes combined)

| Star level | QBs in pool | Qualified (6+ GS in Yr 1 or Yr 2) | Hit rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-star | 12 | 7 | **58.3%** |
| 4-star | 74 | 16 | **21.6%** |
| 4+5-star combined | 86 | 23 | **26.7%** |
| 3-star | 444 | 26 | **5.9%** |
| 2-star | ~234–249 (see Part 2 caveats) | 0 | **0%** |
| 1-star | 0 — no such pool exists | — | n/a |
| Unrated / 0-star | 0 — no such pool exists | — | n/a |

The gradient is steep and monotone: a 5-star QB was roughly **10×** as likely as a 3-star to start 6+ games within his first two seasons, and a 4-star roughly **3.7×** as likely. In absolute terms the 3-star pool produced almost as many early starters (26) as the 4+5-star pool (23) simply because it is ~5× larger — but per-player, the hit rate collapses by star level, and **no 2-star QB in any of the three classes met the test**. There is no 1-star or unrated pool to measure: the 247 Composite QB lists for 2022–2024 contain no 1-star QBs and no 0.0000/unrated entries at all (the lowest-rated QBs, ~0.7000–0.7100, are 2-stars). Class-by-class, the 2024 blue-chip class hit best (34.4% for 4+5★) and 2022 worst (15.4%), while 3-star hit rates were flat (7.2% / 6.0% / 4.1%).

**On 1-star and unrated QBs (explicit, per the brief):** the 247 Composite QB lists for all three classes contain **no 1-star quarterbacks and no 0.0000-rated/unrated entries**. **Denominator caveats:** the 2022 list was paginated in full (pages 1–6; page 7 empty) and its counts are exact. For 2023, the 3-star count (133) is exact, but the final page of 2-stars (page 5) could not be retrieved — the fetch failed and, per instruction, was not retried; page 6 returned empty, confirming the list ends at page 5. For 2024, page 5 is the last page (page 6 empty) and its ~40 entries were estimated from page length; the 2★ total (~63) and class total (~240) are approximations. These gaps affect only the 2★ denominators — and since zero 2-stars qualified, they do not affect any hit-rate conclusion beyond the second decimal of a 0% rate.

### (d) How non-qualifiers were scanned (so completeness can be judged)

1. **Full denominators from 247:** every page of each class's Composite QB list was read (2022 in full; 2023 through page 4 plus the counted portion of page 5's range; 2024 through page 4 with page 5 estimated), producing the complete name/school lists retained in the research notes (`notes/2022-part2-names.md`, `notes/denominators.md`). Every name on those lists was considered.
2. **Season-level screen:** Sports-Reference CFB season passing tables (e.g., https://www.sports-reference.com/cfb/years/2023-passing.html) for 2022–2025 were used to identify which QBs actually played substantial snaps in each season; any 3★/2★ QB with meaningful attempts in his Year 1 or Year 2 became a verification candidate.
3. **Team-starter screen:** candidates were cross-checked against known starting-QB situations at every signee school (e.g., a 3★ signee could only qualify if his school's starter was hurt/benched — each such case was checked individually).
4. **Individual verification:** every plausible candidate (~45 players) was verified individually via Wikipedia GP/GS tables, The Football Database G/GS, Sports-Reference game logs, and official school bios. Verified near-misses include Grayson Loftis (Duke, 2023 class — 5 starts in 2023, one short), Dylan Rizk (4), Taron Dickens (4, FCS), Ethan Vasko (4 in Year 2; his 12-start season was Year 3), Daniel Beale (4), Kevin Jennings (2 in-window; his breakout was Year 3), John Mateer, Maddux Madsen, Caden Veltkamp, Owen McCown (all ≤2 in-window), and 2-star Jack Layne (Idaho — 1 start in Year 1, 1 in Year 2; full-time starter only from Year 3). One name raised by the scan, Mitch Griffis, was excluded after verification showed he is a **2020** recruit (his Wake Forest starts came in his third/fourth college seasons) and thus outside the 2022–24 scope entirely.

Residual risk: a 3★/2★ QB at an FCS school with poor stats coverage could theoretically be missed; the FCS candidates surfaced by the scan (Hurst, Dickens, Layne) were all checked against official school bios, and Hurst is included above.
